What is StylersIn?

StylersIn connects you with verified barbers, hair stylists and nail technicians near you, so you can find, message and book from your phone.

Which services can I book?

Book haircuts, braiding, weaves, locks, crochet, hair dyeing and treatment, makeup, manicures, pedicures, nail services and bridal styling.

Will the styler come to my home?

You can discuss the service location with your styler before confirming your booking.

Are the stylers verified?

Stylers on the app are verified, helping you book with more confidence.

How do I pay?

Pay securely inside the app after arranging your appointment.

How do I join as a styler or shop owner?

Use the “Sign up as a styler” link to get started and create your profile or shop.
